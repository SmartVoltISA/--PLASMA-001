# Mechanism Ablation V2

## Purpose

Controlled diagnostic ablation of the frozen Ω-PLASMA-001 simulator. This experiment does **not** modify or replace the preregistered confirmatory result. It asks which model mechanisms are necessary for the observed post-deletion vacancy response.

## Protocol

- Seeds: 120–179 (60 matched seeds).
- Same initial random seed for every variant.
- T=1000, deletion at t=500.
- Same frozen thresholds and PASS definition as PREREGISTRATION_V2.
- Variants:
  - FULL — frozen model.
  - NO_ADAPT — remove adaptive strengthening; retain decay.
  - NO_REPULSION — remove hard-core repulsion.
  - NO_SPRING — remove relation-weighted preferred-distance spring.
  - NO_ADAPT_NO_REPULSION — remove both adaptation and repulsion.
- Connectivity was evaluated with the same lambda2 criterion.

## Results

| Variant | PASS | Rate | Mean vacancy fraction | Connectivity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FULL | 42/60 | 70.0% | 0.802 | 60/60 |
| NO_ADAPT | 43/60 | 71.7% | 0.815 | 60/60 |
| NO_REPULSION | 45/60 | 75.0% | 0.843 | 60/60 |
| NO_ADAPT_NO_REPULSION | 46/60 | 76.7% | 0.830 | 60/60 |
| NO_SPRING | 1/60 | 1.7% | 0.044 | 60/60 |

## Paired comparison with FULL

Using the same 60 seeds:

- NO_ADAPT: +1 PASS; 4 gains / 3 losses relative to FULL.
- NO_REPULSION: +3 PASS; 8 gains / 5 losses.
- NO_ADAPT_NO_REPULSION: +4 PASS; 8 gains / 4 losses.
- NO_SPRING: -41 PASS; 0 gains / 41 losses.

## Interpretation

The strongest result is the **NO_SPRING** ablation. Removing the relation-weighted spring almost completely eliminates the vacancy response, while connectivity remains intact. This separates two behaviors that the PASS criterion combines:

1. global relational connectivity can remain present;
2. the deleted-node vacancy response requires the spring-mediated relational force.

Adaptive strengthening and hard-core repulsion are not individually necessary for the observed response in this diagnostic sweep. Their removal changes the quantitative behavior, but neither reproduces the collapse seen when the spring is removed.

The result therefore supports a narrower mechanistic statement:

> In this synthetic model, the post-deletion vacancy response is primarily generated by the spring-mediated relation geometry, rather than by adaptation or hard-core repulsion alone.

This is a **model-internal causal diagnostic**, not evidence that the same mechanism operates in real plasma, dark matter, gravity, or astrophysical systems.

## Status

The confirmatory protocol remains unchanged. The ablation is diagnostic evidence used to identify the active mechanism before the final RESULT/DECISION layer.
