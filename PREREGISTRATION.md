# Ω-PLASMA-001 — Preregistration

## 1. Objective
Determine whether a dynamic graph of local interactions can generate and maintain a stable collective structure, including a persistent vacancy after deletion of one node.

## 2. Hypotheses

### H1
A self-organized structure can emerge from local dynamic interactions, and deletion of one internal node can leave a stable structural defect for a finite observation window.

### H0
The system cannot maintain a persistent vacancy under the preregistered conditions; after deletion it relaxes to a defect-free equilibrium or loses structural integrity.

## 3. Model class
- N = 25 nodes.
- Continuous 2D positions.
- Weighted dynamic graph W_ij.
- Local coupling decreases with distance.
- Link weights adapt continuously from local interaction.
- Weak stochastic perturbation is allowed.
- A weak confinement term prevents unbounded drift.
- No rule explicitly creates a target chain, ring, vacancy or other desired geometry.

## 4. Experimental groups

### A — Baseline
No node deletion.

### B — Single vacancy
After formation of the stationary regime, remove one internal node.

### C — Multiple deletion
Remove 2–3 nodes to estimate the structural failure threshold.

### D — Noise sensitivity
Repeat A/B over predefined noise amplitudes.

## 5. Primary metrics
1. Vacancy lifetime.
2. RMS displacement of surviving nodes relative to the pre-deletion state.
3. Graph connectivity.
4. Mean and variance of link weights.
5. Structural order parameter.
6. Time to loss of the collective state.

## 6. PASS criterion
PASS requires all of the following in a preregistered intervention run:
- a stable collective structure exists before deletion;
- the deleted node creates a localized defect;
- the vacancy persists for the full observation window;
- the surviving structure remains globally connected;
- no post-hoc parameter change is used to obtain the result.

A failure of any required condition is recorded as FAIL for that run.

## 7. Controls
- Same initial conditions for matched baseline/intervention pairs.
- Independent random seeds across repetitions.
- No visual matching or manual selection of successful runs.
- Parameters are frozen before analysis.

## 8. Reproducibility
Target: at least 20 independent seeds per parameter regime.
Record software version, random seed, parameters and commit SHA for every run.

## 9. Interpretation boundary
A PASS demonstrates only the behavior of the synthetic model. It does not demonstrate that plasma, dark matter, gravity or any astrophysical phenomenon is generated by the Ω mechanism.

## 10. Decision rule
FACT → CHECK → RESULT → DECISION → FIXATION.
